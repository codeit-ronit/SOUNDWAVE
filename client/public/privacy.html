Privacy and data use

SoundWave is a portfolio audio-streaming demo. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Accounts and Google sign-in

The app stores your account name, email, sign-in identity and session information. Password accounts store a password hash. Google sign-in, when enabled, asks Google for basic identity information: a unique account identifier, name and email. SoundWave verifies Google's signed identity token to sign you in. It does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or other Google content. Google identity and access tokens are not stored in the database.

The Google sign-in script loads when you open an account panel with Google sign-in enabled. Your interaction with Google is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Listening and browser storage

SoundWave stores favorites, chapter progress and listening events for the signed-in account, including an independent guest account when you try the demo. Listening analytics describe activity within SoundWave. HTTP-only cookies keep your session signed in. Browser storage holds playback preferences and explicitly downloaded public audio. Downloads stay on the device across account changes and can be removed in Downloads or through browser settings.

Services and external content

The public demo uses Vercel, Render and Neon for hosting, Redis-compatible infrastructure and PostgreSQL storage. LibriVox supplies audiobook metadata; Internet Archive supplies external audio directly to your browser. These services receive the network requests needed to provide their functions. Operational hosting logs may contain request metadata such as time, address, path and status. SoundWave's application request logger does not record passwords or Google identity tokens.

Retention and control

Registered account data remains until removed by the operator; this demo has no self-service account-deletion page. Guest sessions expire and inactive guest accounts are cleaned up after the configured retention period. Expired sessions and sign-in challenges are cleaned up. Signing out revokes the current SoundWave session; it does not sign you out of Google or erase browser downloads.

For questions or demo-data removal, contact the maintainer through the SoundWave repository. Do not post passwords, sign-in tokens or private personal data in public issues. This page describes the demo's behavior; it does not claim a security or regulatory certification.
